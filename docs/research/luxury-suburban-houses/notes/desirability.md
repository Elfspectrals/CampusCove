# What makes an upscale suburban house "want-to-buy", in real estate and in games

## Which exterior features most increase perceived value or sale price?

### Takeaway
The best-evidenced value drivers are low-cost "first glance" items: the garage door, the entry door, landscaping, door colour, and the overall curb-appeal score. Big-ticket amenities like pools and fire features recover the least money. Curb appeal as a whole is worth about a 7% price premium, and up to 14% in weak markets.

### Cited Findings
- **Garage door replacement** is the top project by return in the 2025 Cost vs Value report (Zonda/JLC, national averages): about $4,672 in cost against about $12,507 added at resale, so roughly 268% recouped. It was 194% the year before. — [JLC Cost vs Value 2025](https://www.jlconline.com/cost-vs-value/2025/); [DASMA copy of report](https://www.dasma.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/CostvsValueReport2025_Winter2025.pdf); [Fixr summary](https://www.fixr.com/resources/cost-vs-value)
- **Steel entry door replacement** in the same 2025 report: about $2,435 cost and about $5,270 added at resale, about 216% recouped. It was 64% in 2022 and 101% in 2023. — [Fixr summary](https://www.fixr.com/resources/cost-vs-value); [JLC 2025](https://www.jlconline.com/cost-vs-value/2025/)
- Zillow cites a different garage-door figure, 349% on $4,317, which is probably another edition of the data. Treat 268% as the 2025 number. — [Zillow](https://zillow.com/learn/best-home-improvements-to-increase-value)
- **NAR/NALP 2023 Outdoor Features report** (cost recovery estimated by Realtors):
  - Standard lawn care service: $415 cost, 217% recovery.
  - Landscape maintenance: 104%. Overall landscape upgrade: 100%. Outdoor kitchen: 100%.
  - In-ground pool (about $90k) and fire features: 56%, the lowest of the 11 projects.
  - 92% of Realtors advise sellers to improve curb appeal before listing.
  - Sources: [NAR report page](https://www.nar.realtor/reports/remodeling-impact-report-outdoor-features); [RISMedia](https://rismedia.com/?p=315051); [Seattle Agent](https://seattleagentmagazine.com/?p=4226); [Houston Agent](https://houstonagentmagazine.com/?p=1299932)
- **Curb appeal premium, measured**: Johnson, Tidwell and Villupuram (J. Real Estate Finance & Economics, 2020) scored Google Street View images of about 90,000 sales with a deep-learning model.
  - Homes with strong curb appeal sold about 7% above comparable homes in the same neighbourhood, and up to 14% in weak markets.
  - The premium is larger in neighbourhoods whose average curb appeal is high.
  - About a third of the premium is tied to the condition of the houses across the street.
  - The model agreed with human raters only about 66% of the time.
  - Sources: [UTA news release](https://www.uta.edu/news/news-releases/2020/02/11/curb-appeal); [The Real Deal](https://therealdeal.com/2020/01/24/curb-your-enthusiasm-homes-with-spruced-up-outsides-sell-at-lofty-premium); [Turf Magazine](https://turfmagazine.com/curb-appeal-now-has-a-number-value)
- A 2020 UConn dissertation reports a 23.37% premium for "good" versus "below-average" curb appeal. This is an outlier against the 7% figure. — [UConn Digital Commons](https://digitalcommons.lib.uconn.edu/dissertations/2510)
- **Landscaping and perceived value** (Behe, Hall et al., 2005; a consumer survey of perceived value, not sale prices):
  - A sophisticated landscape raised perceived value by 5.5% (Louisiana) to 11.4% (South Carolina) over no landscape. A Michigan study using the same method found 12.7%.
  - Plant size (40.2%) and design sophistication (36.5%) were the largest contributors.
  - About $1.09 of perceived value per $1 spent.
  - The often-quoted "15%" figure is questioned by Virginia Tech Extension.
  - Source: [Virginia Tech Extension 426-087](https://pubs.ext.vt.edu/426/426-087.html)
- **Colour**: Zillow's 2018 paint analysis (more than 135,000 photos, 2010–2018 sales) found black or charcoal front doors linked to about $6,271 above expected price, the largest premium in the study. Yellow exteriors were linked to about $3,408 below expected. Zillow says these are correlations, not causes. — [HBS Dealer](https://hbsdealer.com/news/according-to-zillow-these-colors-sell); [Boston.com](https://www.boston.com/real-estate/real-estate-news/2018/06/21/paint-colors-can-impact-homes-sale-price-thousands/)
- **Listing keywords** (Zillow, correlational):
  - 2016: "barn doors" about +13%, "subway tiles", "farmhouse sink"; "quartz" about +6%; "granite" about +4%.
  - 2018: "steam shower" +29%, "herringbone floors" +21%, "hardwood" +10%, "outdoor kitchen" and "heated floors" near the top.
  - Critics say keywords stand in for quality rather than cause the price.
  - Sources: [Zillow 2016 press release](https://zillow.mediaroom.com/2016-04-12-Homes-with-Subway-Tiles-Barn-Doors-or-Farmhouse-Sinks-Can-Sell-for-Up-to-13-Percent-More-and-60-Days-Faster); [Zillow 2018 press release](https://zillow.mediaroom.com/2018-04-04-Homes-with-Heated-Floors-Steam-Showers-or-Outdoor-Kitchens-Sell-for-Nearly-30-Percent-More-than-Expected); [Chicago Agent critique](https://chicagoagentmagazine.com/2018/04/06/listing-descriptions-can-increase-sales-price-29-percent/)
- **Luxury buyers' outdoor requests** (Redfin 2024 survey of about 300 Premier agents): landscaping 69%, indoor/outdoor living 58%, covered patio 46%, pool 33%. The same survey found 54% of buyers unlikely to offer on a luxury home with an outdated kitchen, so the interior is a deal-breaker. — [Florida Realtors](https://floridarealtors.org/news-media/news-articles/2024/06/what-amenities-do-luxury-homebuyers-want); [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20240618460770/en)
- NAHB's "What Home Buyers Really Want" (2024) puts a patio and exterior lighting among the most-wanted outdoor features. — [Builder](https://www.builderonline.com/design/consumer-trends/7-must-have-features-for-2024-new-home-buyers_o)

### Inferences
- In a game, the cheapest geometry pays off most: a distinctive garage door, a statement front door (dark colour, larger than average, sidelights), and full mature landscaping. A pool is a status flourish, not the main value driver.
- Because a third of the curb-appeal premium comes from the houses across the street, a gated estate should keep every lot, including unsold ones, landscaped and maintained. This is a world-design point, not only a per-house one.
- Price ranking for a game, by evidence weight: landscaping and greenery, then the entry (door and porch), then the garage door, then lighting and patio, then the outdoor kitchen, then the pool.

### Gaps
- I found no recent quantified premium for front porches, symmetry, window style, or masonry versus siding. Search results gave only design-blog claims.
- I did not fetch the full 2023 NAR outdoor PDF. Its figures come from trade-press coverage.
- No primary source for Seiler and Bhattacharya on curb appeal was found. The Seiler, Madhavan and Liechty (2012) paper is about restaurants.

## Which features signal luxury at first glance from the street?

### Takeaway
Luxury reads from the street through the arrival sequence: a gate or set-back, a long drive or motor court, an oversized and distinctive entry, natural stone and other heavy materials, mature landscaping, privacy screening, and night lighting. The evidence here is mostly industry and design commentary, not controlled studies.

### Cited Findings
- Designers describe the drive and gate as the start of the home's story. Motor courts with stone or turf inlays and lit driveway edges create a courtyard arrival. — [Joyce Rey (Coldwell Banker Global Luxury)](https://joycerey.com/blog/the-art-of-arrival-designing-driveways-and-entryways-that-impress/)
- In luxury homes, front doors are often larger than average and designed to stand out. Natural stone, marble and granite signal opulence. — [Resident, 2026, Dallas luxury buyers](https://resident.com/home-and-living/2026/04/06/curb-appeal-that-sells-what-luxury-buyers-in-dallas-notice-first)
- Gates combine curb appeal, security and privacy. A common worry is that a gate looks too grand for a suburban setting, so scale has to suit the lot. — [Boston Design Guide](https://www.bostondesignguide.com/?p=42978); [Globus Gates (vendor)](https://globusgates.com/blog/luxury-entry-gates-presidio-heights/)
- Landscaping is the top outdoor request from luxury buyers (69%). — [Florida Realtors / Redfin](https://floridarealtors.org/news-media/news-articles/2024/06/what-amenities-do-luxury-homebuyers-want)
- Plant size, meaning mature trees and shrubs, is the biggest single driver of perceived landscape value. — [Virginia Tech Extension](https://pubs.ext.vt.edu/426/426-087.html)
- BHHS Luxury Collection lists curb-appeal levers for luxury homes, including lighting, entry and landscaping. — [BHHS](https://ignite.bhhs.com/luxury-collection/blog/5-ways-to-enhance-your-luxury-home-s-curb-appeal)

### Inferences
Luxury cues ranked for a 3D game, by how readable they are at street distance:
1. Set-back and lot size: the house sits behind a lawn and a drive.
2. Scale and massing: two storeys, a wide frontage, a tall entry.
3. Entry: double or oversized door, portico or columns, lanterns.
4. Mature trees and hedges, which also give privacy.
5. Heavy materials: stone base, brick, slate or tile roof.
6. A driveway gate or pillars.
7. Night lighting: uplights and lit windows.
8. The garage hidden or side-loaded, with three bays and carriage-style doors.

### Gaps
- There is no quantified study of which first-glance cue weighs most for luxury specifically. Most sources are brokerages or vendors.

## How do games with player housing create desire, status and price tiers?

### Takeaway
Games make houses aspirational through:
- named, location-tied listings at stepped prices, where location carries most of the premium;
- a visible upgrade ladder, where the exterior physically grows;
- an external "rating" that judges design;
- scarce, top-tier landmark properties released as events.

### Cited Findings
- **GTA Online, apartments**: apartments are named listings sold through an in-game real-estate site (Dynasty 8). Prices step by unit inside the same landmark tower: Eclipse Towers Apt 3 costs $500k, Penthouse Suite 2 $905k, Suite 1 $985k and Suite 3 $1.1M. Units are near-identical, so the premium is for a better view or prestige. The tower is modelled on the real Sierra Towers in West Hollywood. — [GTABase Suite 2](https://www.gtabase.com/grand-theft-auto-v/properties/gta-online/eclipse-towers-penthouse-suite-2); [GTABoss](https://www.gtaboss.gg/gta-5-online/properties/high-end-apartments/eclipse-towers-penthouse-suite-1-high-end-apartment); [Gamegeeker](https://gamegeeker.com/news/gta-online-eclipse-towers-real-life-sierra-towers-penthouse-for-sale)
- **GTA Online, mansions**:
  - The "A Safehouse in the Hills" update (10 Dec 2025) added only three mansions in prestige zones, sold through a separate luxury site, Prix Luxury Real Estate: Tongva Estate $11.5M, Vinewood Residence $12.2M, Richman Villa $12.8M.
  - Players who finished earlier missions got a $2M discount.
  - Fully upgraded, a mansion reaches about $18.7M, so the add-ons double as a second price tier.
  - Sources: [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/grand-theft-auto-5/mansions-prices-locations); [GTABase mansions](https://www.gtabase.com/properties/gta-online/types/mansions); [Beebom](https://beebom.com/gta-online-mansion-prices/amp/)
- A GTA guide notes that no residential property pays you just for owning it, so homes sell on status and utility, not yield. — [Geekchamp](https://geekchamp.com/?p=233291)
- **Animal Crossing**:
  - The Happy Home Academy grades the house regularly (ranks B up to S) and rewards matching colour themes. A strong rating gives a small Nook Miles reward.
  - The upgrade ladder works through loans: about seven expansions totalling about 5.7M Bells, and each one visibly enlarges the exterior.
  - Sources: [Nookipedia HHA](https://nookipedia.com/wiki/HHA); [Nookipedia Player house](https://nookipedia.com/wiki/Player_house); [SegmentNext](https://segmentnext.com/animal-crossing-new-horizons-house-upgrades-guide)
- **Stardew Valley**: three farmhouse upgrades from Robin. The first (kitchen) costs 10,000g and 450 wood. The second costs 65,000g and 100 hardwood since v1.6, up from 50,000g and 150. The third (cellar with aging casks) costs 100,000g. Each tier adds a function, not just space. — [Stardew Wiki House](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/House); [Stardew Wiki Cellar](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Cellar)
- **Welcome to Bloxburg**:
  - Pre-built named houses span tiers up to the Bloxington Mansion, a Mediterranean villa. Its listed value is $138k on the wiki and $200k in another guide, so the sources conflict.
  - Higher house value means higher bills, an ongoing cost of prestige.
  - Player-built "ideas" range from about 68k tiny homes to about 557k hillside modern mansions. These are community examples, not official tiers.
  - Sources: [Bloxburg Wiki House](https://welcome-to-bloxburg.fandom.com/wiki/House); [Gamer Journalist](https://gamerjournalist.com/bloxburg-house-ideas/)

### Inferences
Patterns to reuse:
1. **Named models and named listings** in an in-game agency. Use a separate luxury agency for the top tier, as GTA does with Dynasty 8 versus Prix.
2. **Location is the price multiplier.** The same interior costs more in a prestige zone or a better unit.
3. **A limited, numbered supply** of landmark houses (three GTA mansions).
4. **Upgrades that change the exterior or add function** (Animal Crossing, Stardew): each tier is visible to other players or unlocks a utility.
5. **External design validation**, such as a rating or showcase (the Happy Home Academy).
6. **Upkeep scaling with value** (Bloxburg bills), which acts as a sink and keeps status meaningful.

### Gaps
- No usable sources on The Sims 4 (lot traits, household value) or Brookhaven house tiers were retrieved, and no GDC talk or developer post on housing desire. The Bloxburg wiki returned HTTP 402, so its figures come only from search snippets.

## How do luxury subdivisions avoid "cookie-cutter" streets? (anti-monotony rules)

### Takeaway
US and Canadian codes share a common pattern:
- no same elevation next door or directly across;
- at least one or two different houses between repeats;
- percentage caps on repeats per block;
- a separate colour-repeat rule;
- "different" means real changes to roof form, window and door placement, materials and colours. A mirrored plan or a material swap alone does not count.

### Cited Findings
- **Prairie Ridge North (Hampshire, IL)**: the same elevation needs at least one different house between repeats on the same side of the street, and must be at least one house over across the street. The same siding or masonry colour needs two different-coloured houses between repeats. — [Hampshire IL Ex. K](https://hampshirelprod3.muniweb.com/Departments/Zoning-and-Development/Prairie-Ridge-North-Resubdivision/Ex-K-Anti-Monotony.aspx)
- **Lakemoor, IL**: no home may be similar in appearance to a dwelling adjacent to it or directly across the street. — [Lakemoor code](https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/lakemoor/latest/lakemoor_il/0-0-0-4606)
- **Plano, IL**: the same model may sit next to or across from itself only with substantially different elevations. Identical siding colours are barred next door and across. A lot counts as "across" if it overlaps the opposite lot by 25% or more. — [Plano IL code](https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/planoil/latest/plano_il/0-0-0-6562)
- **Caledon, ON (SouthFields design guidelines)**: no same elevation and colour package next to each other; at most 3 identical elevations per 10 lots in a block, with at least 2 houses between them; each model needs at least two distinctly different elevations. — [Caledon SouthFields ADG s.3](https://www.caledon.ca/en/town-services/resources/Documents/business-planning-development/SouthFieldsADG-Section3.pdf)
- **Edmonton-area guidelines**: "different" means a significant change in roof slopes, size and placement of windows and doors, colours and finish materials. A change of material alone, or reversing the plan, is not enough. (Reported via search summary; the exact document was not opened.)
- **Carol Stream, IL (§6-8-2)**: no similar front facades on adjacent lots, and a cap on similar facades per group. A roof-line difference counts only with a significantly enlarged soffit plus columns at least 6" wide or another architectural feature. — [Carol Stream code](https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/carolstream/latest/carolstream_il/0-0-0-1774)
- **Ringwood, IL (2024)**: anti-monotony factors include garage orientation (front or side); main roof pitch at least 6/12; side-load garages must step the end bay at least 4 ft. — [Ringwood ordinance 24-02-02](https://www.ringwood-il.us/sites/default/files/%5Bordinance%5D/24-02-02.pdf)
- **Diamondhead, MS**: compatibility factors are roof pitch, roofing materials, exterior finishes, garage orientation, porches and dormers, and building orientation. — [Diamondhead ch.11](https://www.zoneomics.com/code/diamondhead-MS/chapter_11)
- **Other codes**:
  - Canal Winchester, OH: 6:12 main roof, 4:12 allowed on porches and dormers; front-load garages must carry front-elevation detailing. — [Canal Winchester](https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/canalwinchester/latest/canalwinchester_oh/0-0-0-37525)
  - Middletown, OH: architectural review board approval before a permit; 5:12 minimum roof. — [Middletown](https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/middletownoh/latest/middletown_oh/0-0-0-46099)
  - Baldwin Park, CA: roof form varies every 50 ft. — [Baldwin Park](https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/baldwinpark/latest/baldwinpark_ca/0-0-0-19917)
  - Herriman, UT: single-family design standards. — [Herriman](https://herriman.gov/uploads/files/1062/SingleFamilyDesign.pdf)
- **Apex, NC**: avoid monotony by varying detail, form and siting. No numeric rule. — [Apex](https://apexnc.org/DocumentCenter/View/569)

### Inferences
A ready-to-code rule set for the game estate (Cove Heights):
- No two adjacent lots, and no lot directly across or overlapping by 25% or more, share the same model, elevation and palette.
- Same model: at least 1 different house between repeats on the same side of the street. Same colour package: at least 2 between.
- At most 3 of any one elevation per 10 lots.
- Each model ships with at least 2 elevations that differ in at least two of these axes: roof form or pitch, entry (porch or portico), window rhythm, primary material, garage orientation. A mirrored plan or a colour swap alone does not count.
- The same varied axes fit the modular-builder approach: roof, porch, garage and material are each a parameter.

### Gaps
- No private HOA or master-planned luxury design guideline (Irvine Ranch, Summerlin and similar) was retrieved. The rules above are municipal or Canadian subdivision rules, which developers typically copy into their own covenants.

## What should a game vary per house, and how should each model be named and marketed?

### Takeaway
Vary five or six readable axes per house, and sell each as a named model with a one-line hook and a "signature feature". Price by tier (size), location (prestige lot) and scarcity.

### Cited Findings
- Real anti-monotony codes treat roof form and pitch, window and door placement, materials, colours, porches and dormers, and garage orientation as the axes that make a house "different". — [Diamondhead](https://www.zoneomics.com/code/diamondhead-MS/chapter_11); [Ringwood](https://www.ringwood-il.us/sites/default/files/%5Bordinance%5D/24-02-02.pdf); [Caledon](https://www.caledon.ca/en/town-services/resources/Documents/business-planning-development/SouthFieldsADG-Section3.pdf)
- Listing language shows that named features sell: "barn doors", "farmhouse", "craftsman", "steam shower", "outdoor kitchen". — [Zillow 2016](https://zillow.mediaroom.com/2016-04-12-Homes-with-Subway-Tiles-Barn-Doors-or-Farmhouse-Sinks-Can-Sell-for-Up-to-13-Percent-More-and-60-Days-Faster); [Zillow 2018](https://zillow.mediaroom.com/2018-04-04-Homes-with-Heated-Floors-Steam-Showers-or-Outdoor-Kitchens-Sell-for-Nearly-30-Percent-More-than-Expected)
- Games sell named listings with clear names plus a location: "Richman Villa", "Tongva Estate", "Eclipse Towers Penthouse Suite 3", "Bloxington Mansion". — [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/grand-theft-auto-5/mansions-prices-locations); [GTABase](https://www.gtabase.com/grand-theft-auto-v/properties/gta-online/eclipse-towers-penthouse-suite-2)

### Inferences
Prioritised feature list for a "want-to-buy" game house, highest impact first:
1. **Landscaping package**: mature trees, hedges, flower beds, lawn edging. Highest evidence, and cheap to build.
2. **Statement entry**: an oversized door in a dark or accent colour, sidelights or transom, lanterns, a porch or portico with columns.
3. **Garage**: carriage-style doors, 2 or 3 bays, side-load at the top tier.
4. **Roof form**: hip, gable, mansard or flat-modern, with dormers. This is the strongest silhouette difference from the street.
5. **Primary material and palette**: brick, stone base, siding or stucco, plus trim colour. Use 3 or 4 curated colour packages per style and avoid yellow body colours.
6. **Night lighting**: path lights, uplights, lit windows.
7. **Signature feature**, one per model: turret, balcony over the entry, wraparound porch, pool and cabana, motor court with fountain, rooftop terrace.
8. **Arrival and privacy**: a gate or pillars and a long drive, reserved for the top tier.
9. **Showcase interior**: an updated kitchen is a deal-breaker for real luxury buyers (54%), so top tiers need a hero kitchen and primary suite.

Tiering and marketing:
- **Price tiers** step by size and features (Bloxburg, GTA), with location multipliers for cul-de-sac, hilltop or waterfront lots (GTA).
- **Landmark houses**: one to three numbered, one-of-a-kind houses at the top.
- **Upgrade ladder**: each upgrade (pool, garage bay, extension) is visible to other players (Animal Crossing).
- **Upkeep** scales with value (Bloxburg).
- **Optional estate design rating** in the style of the Happy Home Academy.
- **Naming**: "[Style] [Type]" plus a lot code (for example "Colonial Villa, A-02"), sold through an in-game luxury agency with a one-line hook and a listed signature feature, as GTA's Prix site does.

### Gaps
- There is no data on how players value specific features in life-sim games, such as conversion or price-elasticity studies. The recommendations above are inferred from real-estate evidence and game patterns.
