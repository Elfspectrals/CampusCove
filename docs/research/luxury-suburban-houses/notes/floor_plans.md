# Floor plans and room programs of upscale US suburban single-family houses (300-550 m², two storeys, 3-5 bedrooms)

Units: imperial values from sources, metric conversions added (1 ft = 0.3048 m, 1 sq ft = 0.0929 m²). Conversions are arithmetic, not from sources.

## Ground-floor program: rooms, sizes, adjacencies

### Takeaway
Upscale two-storey plans put a foyer at the front door with the main stair, formal dining (and/or a living room or study) on either side of it, an open great room + island kitchen + breakfast nook across the back facing a covered porch, and a service strip (walk-in pantry, mudroom, laundry, powder room) between kitchen and garage. Many luxury plans also put a guest bedroom with full bath, or the primary suite itself, on the ground floor.

## Staircases: types, placement, widths

### Takeaway
Use a 36 in (914 mm) code minimum width, but build luxury stairs at about 1.1-1.2 m. Put the main stair in the foyer (straight run on the centre-hall axis, or a curved/L stair in a two-storey foyer). Larger plans add a back stair near the kitchen.

### Cited Findings
- IRC residential stair rules:
  - Minimum clear width: 36 in (914 mm)
  - Handrails may project no more than 4.5 in on each side
  - Maximum riser: 7.75 in (196 mm); minimum tread: 10 in (254 mm)
  - Minimum headroom: 6'8" (2,036 mm)
  - Landings: at least 36 in deep in the direction of travel
  
  One web page labelled "IRC 2024" lists 8.25 in risers and 9 in treads, which contradicts the other sources and is probably unreliable. — [2018 Residential Stair Guide (Timnath, CO)](https://timnath.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/2018-RESIDENTIAL-STAIR-GUIDE.pdf); [Placer County Residential Stairs](https://www.placer.ca.gov/DocumentCenter/View/3303/Residential-Stairs-PDF)
- Two sets of stairs (one off the foyer, one off the kitchen) in a 6,242 sq ft luxury plan. — [Architectural Designs 36679TX](https://www.architecturaldesigns.com/house-plans/luxury-5-bed-euro-style-house-plan-with-two-stairs-under-6500-square-feet-36679tx)
- "Impressive Grand Hall and Staircase" visible on entry in a 5,351 sq ft luxury plan. — [ThePlanCollection 20866](https://www.theplancollection.com/house-plans/home-plan-20866)
- In center-hall colonials the hall and stair run straight back from the entry. — [Architectural Designs 32510WP](https://app.architecturaldesigns.com/house-plans/center-hall-colonial-32510wp)
- Two-storey entries that lead to the stair are common in modern-farmhouse plans. — [Houseplans.com SP368190](https://www.houseplans.com/plan/2761-square-feet-4-bedroom-3-5-bathroom-3-garage-modern-farmhouse-sp368190)

### Inferences
- Geometry for a 2.74-3.05 m ground floor (9-10 ft) plus about 0.3 m of floor structure:
  - Total rise about 3.05-3.35 m, so 16-18 risers of about 190 mm and 15-17 treads of about 280 mm.
  - A straight run is about 4.5 m long and 1.1 m wide.
  - A U or L stair with a mid landing fits in about 2.4 x 3.0 m.
- The 1.1-1.2 m width is my own suggestion for a luxury feel and for game-camera clearance; no source gave a luxury stair width.

### Gaps
- No source gave typical widths for curved or grand stairs, or for back stairs.

## Garages: 2- and 3-car sizes and how they connect

### Takeaway
A 2-car garage is about 6.7-7.3 m square (22-24 ft). A 3-car garage is about 9.4-11.3 m x 6.4-7.3 m. It is entered from a mudroom off the kitchen; a bonus room above it or a breezeway to a detached third bay are common luxury variants.

Single door 9' x 7' (2.74 x 2.13 m); double door 16' x 7' (4.88 x 2.13 m). — [Angi: 3-car garage dimensions](https://angi.com/articles/3-car-garage-square-footage.htm); [Homenish: standard garage dimensions](https://homenish.com/?p=2699)

## Features buyers expect inside at this price point

### Takeaway
Buyers expect 9-10 ft ground-floor ceilings (with volume or two-storey spaces at the great room or foyer), a kitchen island with walk-in pantry and eating space, a laundry room, walk-in closets, an office or flex room, and a direct link to a covered porch or patio. Two-storey foyers are divisive but more accepted at higher price points.

### Cited Findings

## Simplifications that still read as "luxury" when low-poly and mostly empty

### Takeaway
No source addresses game or low-poly interiors directly. The cues below are inferred from the features that recur in the plans and surveys above.

### Inferences
- Architectural volume reads as luxury even in empty rooms: high ceilings, one double-height space with an overlooking gallery, a wide straight or L stair facing the front door, and wide openings (cased openings or column pairs about 2.4-3.0 m wide) instead of doors between public rooms.
- Use oversized glazing: a wall of tall windows or sliding glass doors from the great room to a covered porch.
- Build in fixed elements players do not furnish: the kitchen island, a fireplace with mantel, a tray-ceiling inset, and closet and pantry doors. These signal function cheaply.
- Use material contrast in a few flat colours: wide-plank wood floor on the ground floor, a stone or tile band in the kitchen and baths, white trim (baseboard and crown as separate trim nodes).
- Keep many named rooms (pantry, mudroom, butler's pantry, walk-in closets) even if tiny. The room count signals luxury more than finish detail does.
- Keep primary-suite rooms large (bedroom about 25 m², bath about 20 m²) so players can furnish them.

### Gaps
- No published source on how luxury reads in low-poly or game interiors was found; this section is design inference.
